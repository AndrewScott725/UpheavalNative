# Ranged wall balance fix — 2026-09-27

- Invading archers may engage an intact perimeter wall from at most 7 cells; defending archers retain their 8-cell range.
- Invader-to-wall range is measured to the point on the parapet directly opposite the attacker, rather than the wall midpoint.
- Defending wall archers can answer exterior attackers anywhere along their wall when the attacker is within perpendicular range.
- The same rules now apply to surrounding-fiefdom battles.
- Archer attack animation follows authoritative combat state, eliminating the visual loop where archers fire forever when no valid target is actually being damaged.
- Interior/building combat ranges are unchanged.
