# First-engagement arrival countdown

The incoming War Camp countdown now represents the time until the first troop in the army can legally engage.

- Melee-only army: T-0 when the leading infantry reaches the wall.
- Ranged or mixed army: T-0 when the leading invading archers enter their legal wall firing range.
- Archers never fire before T-0.
- At T-0 archers can fire immediately if in range.
- Infantry in a mixed army continues from the archer firing line to the wall at normal approach speed after T-0.
- Home and surrounding-fiefdom battles use the same timing.
