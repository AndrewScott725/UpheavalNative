# Ranged defense reciprocity + repeated placement

- Invading ranged units attacking an intact wall can never occupy a one-way firing pocket.
- Every defending archer stationed on that same wall may answer an invading archer that is within the legal invading wall-attack envelope.
- For each wall, the two connected corner turrets split the frontage. The appropriate adjacent turret is guaranteed to be able to hit an invading archer that is already close enough to damage that wall, even when normal radial turret geometry would otherwise leave a dead zone.
- The same rules are applied to surrounding-fiefdom battles.
- Successful local courtyard building placement now plays `audio/building_place_click.wav`.
- After placement, the currently selected building remains in hand for repeated placement while it remains buildable. It is cleared only when the next copy is locked/unaffordable or construction is temporarily locked by the pre-BEGIN countdown.
