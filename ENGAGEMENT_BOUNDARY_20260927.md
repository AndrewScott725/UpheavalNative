# Exterior engagement boundary update — 2026-09-27

- The incoming countdown remains time to the first legal engagement.
- Defenders may not damage an exterior invading group before that group itself is legally able to attack.
- Exterior ranged invaders become targetable exactly when they enter their legal wall-attack envelope.
- Exterior melee invaders become targetable exactly on physical wall contact.
- The rule is per invading group, so a mixed army reaching T-0 through its archers does not make trailing infantry targetable early.
- Home and surrounding-fiefdom wall/turret defense use the same rule.
- Once an invader is inside the fiefdom, normal interior defender range rules apply.
